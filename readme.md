Falling Blocks is a falling-block idler game that follows the exact The Tetris Company guidelines.

The game plays itself in Falling Blocks in a small window on the desktop, just like TapTapBaby in my other project.

You can earn different skins as you level up.  The game restarts automatically when the AI loses.

Train the AI with your points to make it smarter as it starts learning to do Tetris Back 2 Backs, T-Spin Back 2 Backs, Perfect Clear, Triple T-Spins, and quirky rotation tricks from The Tetris Company guidelines handbook.

Provide the ultimate level when player reaches 500,000 score in accumulation with the tetris grand master blind game shown here: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ij4smVur4EY


Allow different resolution scaling of the game just like TapTapBaby.

Implementation is available. See [PLAYING.md](PLAYING.md) for launching, controls, training, and the added 8-bit Classic mode; see [RULES.md](RULES.md) for the implemented rules and compatibility limits.

New games start in NES Classic. Modern unlocks at 50,000 lifetime points, followed by Blind Grandmaster at 500,000. Version 1.3 adds Handheld, Terminal, and Sunset skins, level-based retro palettes, and separate Classic/Modern AI practice. Completed lines score immediately and wait for typing or a left/right/middle mouse click anywhere on the desktop.
